How it works

Three steps. Ninety seconds.

1

Highlight a name

On WhatsApp Web, email, or any page — highlight a customer name and right-click. Select "Add to Spoke Bridge." Repeat for every name on the list, or paste them all at once.

2

Names match instantly

The extension matches each name against your master customer database using fuzzy search. Handles typos, abbreviations, and Romanian diacritics. Multiple locations? It asks you to pick.

3

Export and go

One click exports a route-ready CSV. Upload it to Spoke, Circuit, or any route planner. Your drivers are on the road while everyone else is still copying addresses.

Features

Built for dispatchers, not developers.

Fuzzy name matching

Handles typos, abbreviations, and partial names. "Taco" finds "TACO SALIS SRL (CITY PARK MALL)."

Romanian-ready

Diacritics normalized automatically. ș, ț, ă, â, î all match their ASCII equivalents.

Zone grouping Pro

Stops auto-sort into delivery zones by city. Export one CSV per zone — one file per driver.

Works with Spoke & Circuit

Exports in native Spoke/Circuit CSV format with coordinates, notes, and all columns preserved.

No cloud, no account

Everything runs locally in your browser. Your customer data never leaves your computer.

Excel & CSV import

Upload your master customer list as .xlsx, .xls, or .csv. Map columns once, matched forever.
